Step 3 — Identify Number Patterns

The numbers that appear are only certain combinations, especially:

  • 88 * 99 * 44 * 55 * etc.

It’s clear that:

  • 88 * 99 appears most dominant and likely represents a binary value.

From observing the pattern, the following mapping was carried out:

1
2

88 = 0
99 = 1

Other numbers like 44 , 55 , etc. are ignored (noise).
